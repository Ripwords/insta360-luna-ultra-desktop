## Viewfinder and HUD

The live viewfinder shows a HUD with recording time, storage remaining,
resolution, and battery. Zoom runs 1×–12×, and there are six capture modes —
Video, Pure, Slow-mo, Photo, Pano, and Timelapse. Photo and video capture are
both one tap.

## Pro bar

An always-visible strip over the viewfinder, grouped into three chips:

- **Exposure** — ISO, shutter, EV, and white balance.
- **Look** — colour mode, the Leica and cinematic filters, and filter strength.
- **Format** — resolution, framerate, and aspect ratio.

## Gallery

A date-grouped grid with photo/video filtering and three thumbnail sizes.
Opening an item shows a full-screen preview with metadata and keyboard
navigation.

## Multi-select

Click a thumbnail to toggle it, shift-click for a range, or select a whole day
at once. Select-all covers the current filter. Selecting anything brings up a
floating action bar for downloading or deleting the selection.

## Downloads

Downloads run as a background queue with per-file progress, streamed straight
from the camera to your Downloads folder.

## Watermark

Photos are watermarked on download with the genuine Insta360 Luna Ultra asset,
placed according to the camera's real aspect-ratio layout table. Video
watermarking is not implemented yet — videos transfer untouched.

## Delete

Deleting removes files from the camera's own storage over the control channel.
This is permanent, and the app asks for confirmation first.

## 3D showpiece

The camera is rendered from its own hi-fi 3D scan, with orbit controls, in
black or white to match the current theme.

## Themes

Two colourways are available: Arctic (light) and Midnight (dark).
