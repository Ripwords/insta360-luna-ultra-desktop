---
title: Use as a webcam (OBS)
description: Send the camera's live preview to Zoom, Meet, Teams or Discord through OBS and its Virtual Camera.
---

Webcam mode serves the camera's live preview on a local address. OBS reads that
address as a Media Source, and OBS's Virtual Camera then shows up as a webcam in
your video-call app. The camera connects over Wi-Fi, so the app has to be
connected to it first (see [Connecting](/docs/connecting)).

## Install OBS

Download OBS Studio from [obsproject.com](https://obsproject.com) and install
it.

## Turn on Webcam mode

1. Open the **Camera** page.
2. Switch on **Webcam mode**, below the capture bar.
3. Select the copy button next to the address. It looks like
   `http://127.0.0.1:49283/webcam.ts`. Copy the address from the panel rather
   than typing it, because the port differs if 49283 is already in use.

The status line under the address reads **Streaming** with the picture size
once video is flowing. Before that it reads **Waiting for camera**, and it reads
**Camera disconnected** if the connection is lost. If the camera stops sending
video, it shows the message described under
[Keep the camera awake](#keep-the-camera-awake).

Webcam mode keeps running when you move to another page. It stops when you turn
it off or the camera connection drops for good. If the app reconnects, Webcam
mode resumes.

## Add it to OBS

1. In OBS, under **Sources**, select **+** and choose **Media Source**.
2. Untick **Local File**.
3. Paste the address into **Input**.
4. Untick **Restart playback when source becomes active**.
5. Select **OK**.

You can add, remove and re-add the source at any time. OBS picks the stream up
from the next clean frame.

## Start the Virtual Camera

In OBS, select **Start Virtual Camera**. Then pick **OBS Virtual Camera** as the
camera in Zoom, Meet, Teams or Discord.

## Keep the camera awake

The camera stops sending video if its screen goes to sleep. Before a call:

- On the camera, set **Screen Auto Sleep** and **Auto Power Off** to their
  longest value, or to Off.
- Keep the camera on power for long calls.

If the app shows "The camera stopped sending video. Its screen may have gone to
sleep. Tap the camera's screen to wake it.", tap the camera's screen. The app
also retries on its own a couple of times, but waking the screen is what
reliably brings the picture back.

## Gallery and downloads

The camera can't stream and transfer files at the same time. While Webcam mode
is on, the Gallery and Downloads pages show "Webcam mode is on. The camera can't
stream and transfer files at the same time." with a **Turn off webcam mode**
button. Thumbnails that are already loaded stay visible, and the file list still
loads, but new thumbnails, opening media and downloads wait until you turn
Webcam mode off.

If a download is running when you try to switch Webcam mode on, the app asks you
to finish or cancel it first: "Finish or cancel your downloads first." It never
cancels anything for you.

## Limits

- No audio. The preview carries video only, so pick a microphone in your call
  app separately.
- Resolution is the preview's, around 720p, not the camera's recording
  resolution.
- There is some delay between the camera and the picture on screen.
- The picture turns portrait in Photo, Video and Pure modes. The size follows
  the camera's current mode, and OBS can crop or scale it.
- File transfers are limited while it is on; see
  [Gallery and downloads](#gallery-and-downloads).
